Research

Machine Learning Computer Vision WashU · Sep 2026 – Present

Machine learning for infant neurodevelopmental assessment

With Geoffrey Goodhill. Annotating anatomical keypoints in infant behavioral assessment videos with CVAT to support computer vision-based analysis, and extending a classification pipeline with Random Forest models for comparison against logistic regression and gradient boosting.

Causal Inference Algorithmic Fairness USC · Feb – Aug 2026

Causal ANOVA decomposition with dependent covariates

With Zijun Gao. Studied causal explainability with Causal ANOVA under dependent covariates and DAG-specified causal assumptions, applied to COMPAS to separate the contributions of race, sex, and age to recidivism outcomes. Resulted in a first-authored arXiv technical report and an open-source Python implementation.

Bayesian Statistics Topology Pitzer College · Jan – May 2026

Asymptotic knot complexity via random grid diagram simulation

With Puttipong Pongtanapaisan. Stochastic simulation of random grid diagrams, with Bayesian quadratic regression and MCMC in PyMC to test the hypothesized O(N²) growth of crossing numbers. Senior thesis work.

Political Science Demographic Modeling CMC · Jan 2025 – Jan 2026

Redistricting reform — LA Redistricting Lab

With Sarah Cannon and Evan Rosenman. Built a reproducible pipeline for voter-file preprocessing, census-linked demographic inference, and BISG race/ethnicity estimation; modeled voter-level outcomes and checked calibration against official election returns. Simulated alternative redistricting plans to evaluate representation outcomes.

Neural Networks Information Theory Scripps College · Sep – Dec 2024

Predictive information in feedforward neural networks

With Sarah Marzen. Applied an information-theoretic framework to assess how efficiently a feedforward ANN allocates computational resources for visual prediction; reproduced V1-like receptive fields and traced training dynamics on the predictive information plane.
